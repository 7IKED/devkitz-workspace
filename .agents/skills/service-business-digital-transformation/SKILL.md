# Service Business Digital Transformation Skill

Transform service businesses into digital-first operations with a proven 5-phase framework. This skill guides creation of accessible websites, local SEO optimization, comprehensive marketing strategies, and sustainable business growth.

## Overview

This skill provides a complete, reusable process for digitally transforming service-based businesses (care services, consulting, trades, professional services). It combines accessible website development, local SEO optimization, marketing asset creation, and business growth strategy.

## When to Use This Skill

Use when: A service business needs digital presence overhaul, building accessible websites for specific audiences, local rankings are critical, coordinated marketing materials needed (web, social, print), structured 5-phase growth plan required, or accessibility is core requirement.

## Core Process: 5 Phases

**Phase 1: Foundations (Weeks 1-2)** - Website audit, business information consolidation, keyword research, Google account setup

**Phase 2: SEO Optimization (Weeks 3-4)** - Google Search Console setup + sitemap submission, Bing Webmaster Tools, Google Analytics, meta-tag optimization

**Phase 3: Local Presence (Weeks 5-8)** - Google My Business profile, local directory registration, review strategy, messaging activation

**Phase 4: Content & Marketing (Weeks 9-12)** - Content calendar, social media channels, posts creation, email marketing foundation

**Phase 5: Monitoring & Optimization (Month 4+)** - Daily task automation, weekly reviews, monthly reporting, quarterly adjustments

**Total Duration:** 12 weeks | **Effort:** 38-53 hours

## Key Deliverables

**Website & Technical:** Barrierefreie (WCAG AA) website, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, .htaccess, meta-tag optimization, structured data

**Marketing Materials:** 8 social media posts, 3 short-form videos, professional logo, business stationery, email templates

**Documentation:** Website analysis, marketing strategy handbook, business setup guide, cost estimate, SEO guides, process documentation, interactive timeline

## How to Use This Skill

### Step 1: Gather Business Information

Collect business name, address, contact info, website URL, business hours, service descriptions, target audience, existing photos, and key differentiators.

### Step 2: Website Development

Follow barrierefreie design: minimum 18px body text, WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1), keyboard accessible navigation, 48x48px touch targets, mobile-first responsive design, <3 second load time.

### Step 3: SEO Setup

Execute in order: Google Search Console (add, verify, submit sitemap), sitemap generation, robots.txt configuration, meta-tags optimization, structured data addition. See `references/seo-setup.md`.

### Step 4: Local Presence

Create Google My Business with accurate NAP, service area, 10+ professional photos, detailed description, attributes, hours, and messaging enabled. See `references/gmb-optimization.md`.

### Step 5: Marketing Strategy

Develop content using 5-phase plan with foundation content (phases 1-2), social media calendar (phases 3-4), ongoing optimization (phase 5+). Create 2+ posts/week.

### Step 6: Monitoring & Reporting

Track monthly KPIs: organic visitors (+50% per quarter), phone calls (+30% per month), website clicks (+25% per month), reviews (+10 per month), rating (>4.5 stars), keyword rankings (top 3 for targets).
